Jibon Borthakur — a name that feels more than a name. Jibon means life, and perhaps it is fitting that a man who gave life to countless songs, memories, dreams, and emotions should be remembered by that name. To millions, he became Zubeen Garg — the voice, the musician, the composer, the actor, the filmmaker, and above all, Zubeen Daa.

Born on 18 November 1972, Zubeen Garg rose from Assam's music scene to become one of India's most versatile artists. His voice carried the soul of the Northeast to audiences far beyond the region. He found nationwide recognition with “Ya Ali” from the 2006 film Gangster, while his Assamese music — from folk-rooted melodies to soulful ballads — made him a deeply loved cultural figure.

He was called Zubeen Garg, but to many of us, he will always be Jibon — a life that gave life to music.

Zubeen Da passed away on 19 September 2025. Yet his voice did not leave with him. His songs remain in our memories, our celebrations, our loneliness, our childhoods, and in the very soul of Assam. This page is a small tribute to the man whose music continues to give life to millions of hearts.
